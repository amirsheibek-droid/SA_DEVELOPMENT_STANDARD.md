Engineer Network Terms

Plain English. These terms apply when a developer, engineer or builder joins the SA Intelligent Designs engineer network for Bedford, Luton, Milton Keynes and the surrounding towns (“the network”). “SA”, “we” and “us” mean SA Intelligent Designs. “You” means the engineer.

The short version
  • Joining is free, and so is connecting with other members.
  • Clients see your skills, never your identity, until they pay a project deposit.
  • All project payments go through SA. SA keeps a 15% platform fee from your share.
  • You're paid within 7 days of each client payment clearing.
  • Clients we introduce are billed through SA for 12 months.
  • You can leave at any time.

1. Joining and verification

Joining the network is free. You'll build a profile of your skills, languages, tools and experience. We review your work and may hold a short technical call before approving you. We can decline or remove a profile at our discretion, for example if information is inaccurate.

2. Your profile and your identity

Once approved, you get a public profile (your “neuron”) showing only your skills, depth ratings, anonymised case study, credentials, availability, nearest town, how you like to work and how you'd like to connect. Your name, email, address, links and rate are never published. They're shared with a client only after that client has paid a deposit for a project with you. You confirm that everything in your profile is true, and you can ask us to update or remove it at any time.

3. How work reaches you

A client requests you through the site. We check the scope with you first, and you can always say no. If you accept, we send the client a quote and a deposit link. When the deposit is paid, we introduce you to the client.

3a. Connecting with other members

Connecting with other members is free. When a member asks to connect with you, we pass on their message and only share contact details if you both agree. Work you find together yourselves is yours. If you team up on a client project that SA introduced, sections 4 to 6 apply to that project.

4. Fees

SA's platform fee is 15% of the agreed project price, taken from the payments clients make through SA. You set your own rates, so include the fee in your pricing. There are no other charges to engineers.

5. Getting paid

Clients pay SA, not you directly. The client's deposit (usually 20% of the agreed price) counts towards the project. We pay you your share of each payment, minus the platform fee, within 7 days of it clearing, or at the milestones agreed for the project.

6. Clients we introduce

For 12 months after we introduce you to a client, all paid work with that client, including new projects, goes through SA. Please don't take payment from an introduced client directly. If you do, the 15% platform fee on that work is still owed to SA.

7. Doing the work

You're an independent contractor, not an employee of SA. You're responsible for your own tax, insurance and equipment, and for delivering work as agreed in the project scope. Unless the project agreement says otherwise, ownership of the work passes to the client once they've paid for it in full.

8. Confidentiality and data

Keep client information, code and data confidential, and use it only for the project. We use your details only to run the network and to introduce you to clients.

9. Leaving the network

You can leave at any time by emailing Contact@samarketingintelligence.com. We'll remove your profile within 5 working days. Projects already in progress, and section 6 for clients already introduced, still apply.

10. Changes and law

We'll email you at least 30 days before changing these terms. These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales.
